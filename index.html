Import Dataset

Add sightings from CSV or JSON. Compatible with NUFORC / Timothy Renner / planetsig formats.
⇪
Drop a .csv or .json file here, or click to choose
Expected columns (any subset):
date · datetime · city · state · country · latitude/lat · longitude/lng/lon · shape · duration · summary/text/description · report_link/url

Large files: up to ~100k rows works comfortably. Data is held in memory (nothing is uploaded anywhere) and clears when you close the tab.

Load Remote Datasets

Public UAP/UFO datasets hosted on GitHub. Pick one, then use the action buttons.
Recommended: use ↗ OPEN ON GITHUB. That takes you to the file's page where there's a clearly-labeled "Download raw file" button. Save the CSV, then come back here and drag it onto ⇪ IMPORT DATA. This path is the most reliable for any file size.

Why not auto-fetch? Browsers block direct fetches to GitHub (CORS). Public CORS proxies exist but are rate-limited, often broken, and choke on files over ~5 MB. They're provided as a fallback but don't expect them to work for the big 40 MB NUFORC dump.

About UAP Archive

A single-file, offline-capable UAP/UFO research terminal. Everything runs in your browser — no data is uploaded anywhere, no accounts, no tracking. Import NUFORC or other CSV/JSON datasets and explore them with rigorous statistics that account for reporting bias, cultural priming, and known stimulus events.

Built-in data: — documented cases across — countries, from the Tulli Papyrus through the 2024 New Jersey drone wave, with — featured cases that include deep-dive satellite maps.

The point of this tool: give the UAP community a way to look at report data honestly. Every chart explains what it measures and what it doesn't. Click any chart element to see the actual reports behind the number.
